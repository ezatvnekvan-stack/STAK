# Refined Nothing styling

Uses the user's earlier dark Home and Sources screenshots as the visual baseline.
Removes diagonal gloss, blue-tinted controls, poster caption frames and pill
backgrounds around text links. Panels use neutral surfaces and thin borders.
Primary actions retain Nothing red; the dock restores the monochrome selection
pill. Poster artwork and provider logos remain prominent. Continue Watching text
is slightly larger, and detail artwork blur is toned down for readable content.

The underlying scrolling, caching, source filters and compact poster menu behavior
remain in place. Cards use standard Material surfaces rather than nested custom
glass clipping/shader layers. The dock keeps a restrained translucent treatment.

CI captures the native reference routes before running the complete functional
suite, uploading those images separately for design review. These are actual
Android captures. They do not certify pixel parity, live providers or sustained
90 Hz on the user's Nothing Phone (3a), Android 16.
